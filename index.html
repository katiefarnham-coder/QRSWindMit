What shape is your roof?
Stand at the street and look at your house — which of these matches what you see?
Hip Roof
All 4 sides slope down. No flat triangular ends.
BIGGEST DISCOUNT
Gable Roof
Triangle shapes visible on the ends — most common style.
Flat / Low Slope
Mostly flat top — common on commercial or modern homes.
Mixed / Combo
Has both hip and gable sections on the same roof.

How is your roof deck nailed down?
This is how the plywood under your shingles is fastened to the frame. Not sure? Pick QRS Standard — that's what we install on every job we do.
QRS STANDARD
8d Nails — Close Pattern
Nails every 4–6 inches. Strongest hold possible.
BIGGEST DISCOUNT
8d Nails — Staggered
Alternating rows of nails. Good hold.
8d Nails — Wider Spacing
Nails further apart. Some discount.
Not Sure
An inspector will check and document this for you.
4
How is your roof attached to your walls?
This is a metal connector inside your attic between the roof frame and the walls. Not sure? Pick QRS Standard — that's what we install on every job.
QRS STANDARD
Double Wraps
Two metal straps wrap fully over the rafter. Strongest possible connection.
BIGGEST DISCOUNT
Single Wrap
One strap wraps over the rafter. Good connection.
Clips / Brackets
Small metal clip on one side only. Some credit.
Toe Nails Only
Just angled nails — common in older homes. No extra credit.
5
Are your windows and doors protected?
Wind entering through openings creates internal pressure that can blow your roof off from the inside. This category has a huge impact on your discount.
QRS RECOMMENDED
Impact-Resistant Glass
Entire home has glass or film rated to stop windborne debris.
BIGGEST DISCOUNT
Hurricane Shutters
Roll-down or accordion shutters on all windows and doors.
Storm Panels
Removable panels stored and installed before a storm.
No Protection
Standard windows with no storm protection installed.
